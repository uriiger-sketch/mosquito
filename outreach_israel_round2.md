## Email 7 — Dr. Oscar David Kirstein

**Subject:** Acoustic mosquito detection by phone: adding Culex perexiguus with your lab's help?

Dear Dr. Kirstein,

I'm developing MosquitoNet, a free, open-source web app that detects mosquitoes by their wingbeat tone using an ordinary phone microphone, with all processing on the device. It identifies likely vector species and reports confirmed detections to a map, with location blurred by about 2.8 km for privacy.

The app has a gap I can't close on my own: it doesn't yet include Culex perexiguus, which Ministry of Health research names as the main West Nile vector in Israel. Would your laboratory be willing to let me record live or freshly trapped Cx. perexiguus and Cx. pipiens with a few phones at measured distances? I would do the recording work myself and share the recordings and results with your lab. Longer term, I'd value your view on whether app detections could be compared with your trap sites for validation. So far the long-range detection has been tested only on simulated recordings.

Code: https://github.com/uriiger-sketch/mosquito

I'd be glad to come to Jerusalem or talk by phone at your convenience.

## Email 10 — Prof. Yossi Yovel

**Subject:** Acoustic mosquito detection on phones: would you critique the method?

Dear Prof. Yovel,

I'm developing MosquitoNet, a free, open-source web app that detects mosquitoes by their wingbeat tone using a phone microphone, with all processing on the device. Your lab's work on insect and plant acoustics, including the 2025 eLife study showing that female moths use plant sounds when choosing where to lay eggs, deals with the same problem I face: telling a faint biological signal apart from real-world noise.

The detector uses a sonar-style harmonic tracker that, in simulation, works at signal levels about 26 dB weaker than before. But it has only been tested on simulated audio, and outdoor noise is where I expect it to break down. Would you, or someone in your lab, be willing to look at the method critically, or advise on how best to validate it in the field? The code is fully open.
